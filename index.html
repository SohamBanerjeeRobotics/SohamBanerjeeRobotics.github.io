01 — About

Where control theory meets real hardware.

I work at the intersection of control theory, robotics, and reinforcement learning — designing systems that don't just simulate well, but move, balance, and navigate in the real world.

My recent research at IIT Bombay, under Prof. Arpita Sinha and Prof. Vivek Sangwan, focuses on underactuated bipedal locomotion with pneumatic ankle push-off — bridging low-level continuous control models with high-level trajectory optimization, validated through both simulation and hardware-in-the-loop testing.

From drones navigating fire-affected environments to mapless mobile robots trained entirely in simulation and deployed on real hardware, I care about building systems that survive contact with reality.
